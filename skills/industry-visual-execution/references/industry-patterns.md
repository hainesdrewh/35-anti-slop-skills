# Industry Layout Patterns

Section-by-section structure per vertical. These are starting points reflecting how each category typically converts best, not rigid templates; adjust based on the specific business's actual PRD from [`project-scoping-and-prd`](../../project-scoping-and-prd/SKILL.md).

## Real estate listing page

1. Full-bleed photo gallery/carousel, high resolution, real navigation (arrows, thumbnails, or dots), not auto-advancing without user control.
2. Sticky summary bar once scrolled past the hero: price, address, beds/baths/sqft, a schedule-showing CTA.
3. Key facts grid: price, beds, baths, square footage, lot size, year built, property type.
4. Description written for the specific property, not a generic listing template ("charming" and "cozy" used on every listing are as much a tell as any AI-slop phrase).
5. Map with real location and nearby points of interest (schools, transit, groceries) where relevant to the buyer.
6. Floor plan if available.
7. Agent/agency contact block with a real photo and direct phone/email.
8. Similar/nearby listings at the bottom, not unrelated content.

## Landscaping / home exterior

1. Hero: full-bleed image or slow background video of real, current, seasonally-appropriate completed work.
2. Trust strip: years in business, licensing/insurance, review rating, directly below the hero.
3. Services as distinct, real sections or pages (see [`local-service-seo`](../../local-service-seo/SKILL.md)), not a single vague "our services" paragraph.
4. Before/after gallery, prominent, not buried below several other sections.
5. Real reviews with names and locations.
6. Clear, tap-to-call-first contact path.

## Restaurant

1. Hero: real photography of the space or a signature dish, not a stock image of unrelated food.
2. Hours, location, and a reservation/order CTA visible without scrolling.
3. Menu as real HTML text (readable, searchable, accessible), with photos of standout dishes, not a photo or PDF of the physical menu as the only version.
4. Atmosphere/story section: what makes this specific restaurant's experience distinct.
5. Real customer photos or press mentions if genuinely available.
6. Map and parking/transit info.

## Professional services (law, accounting, consulting)

1. Calm, restrained hero: a clear, specific statement of what this firm does and for whom, not a generic "excellence in service" tagline.
2. Credentials and specific outcomes (case results, client categories served) prominently, early.
3. Team bios with real photos and real credentials (see [`trust-and-about-content`](../../trust-and-about-content/SKILL.md)).
4. A single clear primary CTA (schedule a consultation), not competing CTAs of equal visual weight.
5. Testimonials/case studies with enough specificity to be credible (see [`review-testimonials-social-proof`](../../review-testimonials-social-proof/SKILL.md)).

## Home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical)

1. Hero with the core value prop and an immediate, prominent phone number/CTA, since a meaningful share of traffic is in an active-problem state.
2. Trust strip: licensed/insured, years in business, rating, directly visible.
3. Services as distinct pages with real pricing ranges where possible (see [`local-service-seo`](../../local-service-seo/SKILL.md)).
4. Service area clearly stated.
5. Reviews, prominent and real.
6. Emergency/same-day availability called out explicitly if true.

## Medical, dental, fitness

1. Hero: real photo of the actual space/team, calm and approachable, not stock photography of unrelated professionals.
2. Booking/appointment path immediately visible.
3. Insurance/payment information clearly stated, since it's often a deciding factor before someone will even consider booking.
4. Provider bios with real credentials.
5. Accessibility given extra weight (contrast, clear navigation, larger tap targets) given the audience.
