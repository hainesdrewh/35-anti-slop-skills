# Contact Page Standards

## Why this matters

The contact page is where a visitor goes specifically because they've decided to reach out, and any friction or ambiguity here (a missing method they prefer, unclear hours, a form with no confirmation) directly costs a conversion at the exact moment intent was highest. A contact page that only offers a form, with no visible email or phone number, unnecessarily excludes visitors who prefer or need those channels.

## Do this

### Multiple real contact methods

Offer more than one way to reach the business where realistic for it: a real phone number as a working `tel:` link, a real email as a working `mailto:` link (see [`mobile-navigation-ux`](../mobile-navigation-ux/SKILL.md)), and a contact form for visitors who prefer not to call or email directly. Don't force every visitor through a form if direct contact info is genuinely available.

### Map embed

For a business with a physical location customers visit, include a real, accurate map embed (not a screenshot) showing the actual address, ideally linking out to get-directions functionality.

### Hours

State current, accurate hours directly on the contact page, matching exactly what's shown elsewhere (Google Business Profile, footer) per the NAP-consistency requirement in [`local-service-seo`](../local-service-seo/SKILL.md). Note any holiday or seasonal exceptions if the business has them, rather than leaving a visitor to discover a closure by showing up.

### Social links

Link to the business's real, active social profiles (see [`trust-and-about-content`](../trust-and-about-content/SKILL.md) for the full standard on this).

### Contact form completeness

If a contact form is included, apply the full standard from [`form-ux-feedback`](../form-ux-feedback/SKILL.md): clear success confirmation after submission, specific inline error messages, and a realistic expectation set for response time ("we typically respond within one business day").

## Never do this

- Never make a contact form the only way to reach the business when a real phone/email are available and should be shown directly.
- Never use a static map screenshot instead of a real, accurate embedded map for a location customers visit.
- Never let the hours shown on the contact page drift out of sync with hours shown elsewhere.
- Never leave a submitted contact form with no clear confirmation of what happens next.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Real phone number present as a working `tel:` link
- [ ] Real email address present as a working `mailto:` link
- [ ] Map embed (not a screenshot) is present and accurate, if the business has a location customers visit
- [ ] Hours are current, accurate, and match every other place they're listed
- [ ] Social links point to real, active profiles
- [ ] Contact form (if present) gives clear success/error feedback and sets a response-time expectation
